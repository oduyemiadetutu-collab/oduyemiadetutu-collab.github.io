Case study: Auto Home Pilot

UI/UX capstone at TSAcademy · Mobile app concept · Figma and FigJam

The problem

Working mothers who juggle a job and a side hustle make hundreds of small household decisions every week. Meals, outfits, chores, errands and kids’ appointments all compete for the same limited attention.

The solution

Auto Home Pilot is an app that automates those decisions and tasks so a busy parent can run the home from one place. The MVP covers six features:

  • Meal planning and shopping list
  • Wardrobe and outfit planner
  • Task and chore prioritization
  • Errand and cleaning outsourcing
  • Kids’ routines and appointment reminders
  • One dashboard that brings it all together

What the interviews showed

  • Decision fatigue is the real burden. The hardest part of the day wasn’t the tasks themselves, it was deciding what to do.
  • Mental exhaustion peaks in the afternoon and evening. That’s when meal and prioritization decisions pile up.
  • Tools are fragmented, not integrated.
  • People rely on memory, phone alarms, WhatsApp notes and Google Calendar, but nothing connects them.

Who I designed for

  • Jane, 35, the working juggler. Full-time job plus a side hustle, exhausted by evening, wants meal, wardrobe and errand automation.
  • Victoria, 28, the full-time caregiver. Runs the household for four children and feels overwhelmed when everything seems equally urgent. She wants help prioritizing.
  • Anita, 27, the new mother. Has a newborn on top of everything else and wants meals, shopping and children’s routines handled with minimal input.

How I worked

  1. Interviewed six mothersI started with real people and listened for their biggest pain points before sketching anything.
  2. Defined three personasJane, Victoria and Anita, all based in Lagos, with working mothers as the primary user.
  3. Scoped the MVPI turned the pain points into six core features.
  4. Designed 25+ screens in FigmaFlows and screens for each feature, planned and mapped with FigJam.
